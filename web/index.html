Models & privacy

Everything runs inside this browser tab (whisper + translation via transformers.js, ONNX Runtime Web). Audio never leaves your device. Models (30–115 MB) are downloaded once and cached for offline use.

Browsers cannot provide a system keyboard. Use the Android app for keyboard dictation inside other apps; here, copy or share the text.
